## Locked decision: new row **OQ-046** "The dungeon break", resolved
1. **The dungeon feeds.** Every death in a dungeon, by any player or NPC (pillar 1), feeds that dungeon's **break counter**. After X deaths the dungeon **breaks**.
2. **The break.** A flood of monsters pours out into the area around the entrance.
3. **Where it hits.** Dungeons sit inside or beside every midsize city or larger (07-dungeons). A village can host one through a settled rift (DNG-014). Few defenders: "hood luck have fun". In a city, **the guards defend it**: NPC guards fight the flood by the same combat rules (pillar 1).
4. **Reputation.** When the dungeon breaks, the characters whose deaths fed it lose reputation with that settlement's faction (→ NPC-015).
5. **Defended.** Once the break is defended successfully, the dungeon's difficulty tier (DNG-009) **resets**, and so does its break counter. The world difficulty (OQ-045, WLD-025) is untouched: it never goes back down.
6. **Not defended.** The monsters take over the area, as in the tug of war (OQ-044, NPC-023): the settlement can be overrun and become ruins, and it can be retaken later.

**Decided in DNG-016** (invent no numbers here):
- X, and whether the counter decays over time like the tier;
- the flood's size, its monster levels and its reach;
- what counts as "defended successfully";
- how much reputation is lost, and whether defending earns some back;
- whether rifts break too.
