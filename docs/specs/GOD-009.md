# GOD-009: Camera can go low and look up at the sky

Source: Lucid, 2026-10-20, playtesting GOD-008: "I would like the right click to be a little bit more towards the sky, like looking up a little more; the angle it's currently capped at feels a little restrictive (I like looking up)."
Milestone: G0 follow-up (playtest round 2)   Depends on: GOD-008 (#46, merged: the orbit, `PLAY_PITCH_MIN/MAX_DEG`, `--yaw`/`--pitch`)
Model: opus. Camera feel.
Branch: god-009-camera-look-up

## Desired result
Dragging with the right mouse button (or the equivalent input) past today's low limit keeps going. The camera sinks toward the ground behind the wanderer and tilts up, until the view looks clearly **above** the horizon, up into the sky where G1's cosmic eye lives (the eye sits about 40° up).
- **At the lowest setting**, the wanderer stays on screen, in the lower part of the frame, and the sky fills most of the view.
- **The camera never goes under the terrain**, even on hills, slopes or next to a cliff.
- **The high end (near top-down) is unchanged.**

## Locked design (view only: `camera_rig.gd`, plus `main.gd` wiring if needed)
1. **Pitch range.** Extend the orbit pitch below today's `PLAY_PITCH_MIN_DEG` (23°), down to about **3–5°** (the camera nearly level with the target).
   - **Tilt up as it sinks:** the look raise grows smoothly as the pitch goes below about 23°, so that at the minimum the view direction points about **20–30° above the horizon**. The target point moves up the screen and the wanderer sits near the bottom of the frame. Use a smooth curve with no kink at 23°.
   - **Tune by captures:** the wanderer stays fully in frame (feet to head) at the lowest pitch at the default zoom of 14 m and at the zoom limits (8 m and 24 m).
2. **Terrain clearance.**
   - The camera's world position keeps at least about 0.6 m above `store.height_at(camera x, z)` and also above the target's own height + 0.3 m.
   - Implementation: give the rig a read-only height lookup, a Callable or the `OgChunkStore`, set by `main.gd`. Views may read rules state.
   - When terrain would block, pull the camera in along its arm (shorten the distance) rather than popping it up. Keep it smooth, with no jitter when walking along a slope.
   - The rig only reads the store; it never writes it.
3. **Vista** (hold V or Y): unchanged. It eases out from the current yaw. Coming back from vista restores the low pitch if that's where the player was.
4. **Input.**
   - The drag direction stays as it is: dragging down raises the camera; Lucid hasn't asked for a change, and the critic confirmed the current signs are consistent. If the existing direction already lowers the camera on drag-up, keep it.
   - Orbit sensitivity is unchanged.
   - The gamepad gets no new pitch binding (out of scope).
5. **Fog and sky:** looking up shows mostly sky.
   - At low pitch the camera sees farther along the ground, so check that the streaming edge stays hidden at the lowest pitch, yaw 0 and 45, play view.
   - If the current depth fog leaves an edge, adjust only the play fog distances in `camera_rig.gd`.
   - The G1 world lab (ART-027, in progress on another branch) moves the fog into a sky-matched post pass and will take this over. Keep changes minimal.
6. **Contracts:** update the numbers in the comment block at the top of `camera_rig.gd`. Report the new numbers for the CLAUDE.md Device-input row (the orchestrator edits CLAUDE.md).
