Branch: god-006-character

## Desired result
The placeholder wanderer stands at spawn in the streamed world. With keyboard and mouse, or a gamepad, Lucid can:
- walk;
- sprint;
- dodge-roll toward the stick or aim;
- swing an attack toward the cursor or right stick.

Cliffs and deep water stop the wanderer with sliding, and shallow water slows them. The camera follows. The rules are the same code an NPC will use: a test drives a second character with no input device through the exact same step.

## Locked design

### Units and timing
1. **Units:**
   - metres and seconds; one tile = 1 m (GOD-005);
   - the rules plane is (x, z), and `Vector2(x, z)` holds it in rules code;
   - `y` is presentation only, from `height_at`.
2. **Tick:** a fixed rules tick `TICK_HZ = 60` in `game/src/rules/constants.gd`. It equals `physics/common/physics_ticks_per_second`; a test asserts the two match. Every duration is a whole number of ticks, `round(seconds * TICK_HZ)`.
3. **Starting values (tunable, as in OQ-021 and OQ-025):**
   - walk 5 m/s, sprint × 1.6;
   - dodge 3 m over 0.35 s (21 ticks);
   - attack 0.4 s (24 ticks), rooted;
   - press buffer 0.2 s (12 ticks);
   - shallow water speed `OgWalkability.SHALLOW_WATER_SPEED`;
   - foot box: a square of half-size 0.3 m centred on the feet point (no longer the sprite-shaped TS box).

### Rules (`game/src/rules/`; plain classes, no Node, no Input, no rendering)
4. **`character.gd` (`OgCharacter`, RefCounted).** The one character shape for players and NPCs:
   - `pos: Vector2`, `vel: Vector2`, and `facing: Vector2` (unit, continuous; no 4-way snapping in 3D);
   - `action` (an enum: IDLE, WALK, ATTACK, DODGE; later HURT and DEATH) and `action_start` (tick);
   - `half_size`;
   - `intent`: `move: Vector2` (magnitude 0..1), `aim: Vector2` (offset from the character in metres), and `buttons` bits SPRINT (held), ATTACK_PRESS and DODGE_PRESS (one-tick edges).
5. **`collision.gd` (`OgCollision`):** `slide_move(ground, pos, half, delta) -> Vector2` and `speed_factor_at(ground, pos)`. These port `collision.ts` exactly, in metres:
   - a half-open box;
   - x first, then z, with sliding;
   - only newly entered tiles are checked, every column or row scanned (no tunnelling);
   - flush stops;
   - the speed factor comes from the tile under the box centre.
   - `ground` is anything with `tile_at(tx, ty) -> int`: `OgChunkStore` in the game, a dictionary-backed fake in tests.
6. **`actions.gd` and `movement.gd`:** `step_actions(chars, tick)` and `step_movement(chars, tick, ground)` port `actions.ts` and `movement.ts`:
   - **Ending and starting actions:** an action ends after its duration. Dodge and attack start only from IDLE or WALK, and dodge wins over attack.
   - **Dodge direction:** the move direction, else the aim, else facing.
   - **Facing when an action starts:** it snaps to the aim when the aim is non-zero (OQ-025).
   - **During actions:** a dodge keeps its velocity and ignores intent. An attack roots the character.
   - **Facing while walking:** it follows the velocity, and holds when idle.
   - **Buffering:** a press in the last `BUFFER_TICKS` of an action survives to start the next one; other presses are dropped.
   - **Speed factor and walls:** they act on displacement only; the stored velocity is never scaled.
   - Keep the TS arithmetic discipline: only `+ - * /`, `sqrt`, `abs`, `floor`, `min` and `max`. A non-finite intent is reported (`push_error`) and treated as zero, never propagated into positions.
7. **`input_latch.gd` (`OgInputLatch`).** It ports `input.ts`. An `InputFrame` holds:
   - `move` and `aim`;
   - `sprint: bool`;
   - u32 `attack_presses` and `dodge_presses` counters.

   It is validated, latched at tick start into one character's intent, and a changed counter sets the press bit once. This is the shape the G3 network input message will carry, so keep it a plain value type.
8. **`sim.gd` (`OgSim`, RefCounted): the host's rules world.**
   - It holds the tick counter, the characters, the store, and one latch per *driven* character (a dictionary from character to latch).
   - `step()` does, in this order:
     1. latch every driven character's input;
     2. `step_actions`;
     3. `step_movement`;
     4. `store.set_anchors(<driven characters' positions>)` and `store.step()`;
     5. increment the tick.
   - No other line knows which character is the local player. Add `you` (the local player's character) only as a field the views read.
   - The spawn is `OgSpawn.find_spawn(seed)` converted to metres (the tile centre).

### Input (`game/src/input/`)
9. **`actions.gd`:** extend `OgActions.ensure()`, keeping the GOD-005 actions:
   - `sprint` on Shift and the left stick click or LB;
   - `dodge` on Space and gamepad B (east);
   - `attack` on the left mouse button and gamepad X (west) or RT.
   - Document the bindings in one comment block.
10. **`local_input.gd` (Node):** builds the `InputFrame` for `you` each physics tick.
    - **Move:** WASD or the arrows or the left stick, with a deadzone, clamped to length 1. Screen-relative directions map to world (x, z) through the camera yaw (fixed north-up in G0, but use the yaw so a later rotating camera just works).
    - **Aim, keyboard and mouse:** the point on the horizontal plane at the character's feet height under the cursor, minus the character position.
    - **Aim, gamepad:** the right stick direction × 3 m. With no stick input, aim is zero, so facing comes from movement.
    - **Device switching:** the most recently used device (any key, mouse motion or click vs any stick or button beyond the deadzone) decides the aim source and which moves count.
    - **Presses:** count them in `_input`/`_unhandled_input` (`is_action_pressed` events, ignoring echoes), so a tap shorter than a tick is never lost. The frame carries the counters.
11. **Mouse capture:** none. The cursor stays visible, since it's an ARPG.

### Views (`game/src/render/`; read rules state, never write it)
12. **`character_view.gd`:** a placeholder wanderer made of primitives (no imported model; the real one is G1):
    - a capsule body and a cone-ish poncho in orange with a gold scarf ring;
    - a darker hood with no face (D6);
    - about 1.7 m tall;
    - feet at `height_at(pos)` plus a small wading sink in water (presentation only).
    - **Facing:** yaw follows `facing`, eased over about 0.08 s for looks; the rules facing is instant.
    - **Readable action states:**
      - walk: a slight bob;
      - sprint: a forward lean;
      - dodge: a forward roll or tumble over its duration;
      - attack: a quick arc swing (a thin curved mesh or trail that flashes in the facing direction).
    - It moves in `_physics_process` with physics interpolation, and calls `reset_physics_interpolation()` on spawn and teleports.
13. **`main.gd`:**
    - Replace GOD-005's debug focus with an `OgSim` plus one character for `you`; the camera follows its view.
    - HUD adds: action, speed, the tile under the feet, and the input device in use.
    - **`--autopilot`:** a scripted `InputFrame` sequence for captures and the G0 E2E: walk east 2 s, sprint 1 s, dodge, attack, walk north 2 s. It goes through the same latch, not around it. Combined with `--capture`, it captures at a given tick (`--capture-tick N`).

### Tests (`game/tests/`)
14. **`test_collision.gd`** ports `collision.test.ts`: flush stops on both axes in both directions, negative coordinates, no tunnelling at high speed, sliding, the outside-corner order, a box straddling two rows, and where the speed factor samples.
15. **`test_character_rules.gd`** ports the movement, action and sprint cases of `movement.test.ts` and `actions.test.ts` in metres and ticks:
    - walk distance after 60 ticks = 5 m on an axis and on a diagonal;
    - sub-unit stick magnitudes;
    - sprint;
    - facing rules;
    - shallow water ×0.6 for walk, sprint and dodge;
    - a dodge into a cliff stops flush and stays DODGE for its duration;
    - attack roots, then walking resumes;
    - the buffer window keeps a late press and drops an early one;
    - non-finite intent is reported, and positions stay finite.
16. **`test_input_latch.gd`:** validation rejections; a changed counter = one press; an unchanged counter = no press; u32 wraparound counts as a change.
17. **`test_one_rule_set.gd`:**
    - an `OgSim` with two characters, one latched from an `InputFrame` and one whose intent the test writes directly (as an NPC brain will), each with the same intent, ends at identical positions, facings and actions after 120 ticks;
    - grep-style assertion: no file in `game/src/rules/` references `Input`, `Node`, `get_tree` or `InputMap` (read the files as text).
18. No pixel assertions.

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1** `bash tools/godot/test.sh` → all PASS, exit 0.
- **AC2** Mutations, each temporary and reverted:
  - remove the "only newly entered tiles" start offset in `slide_move` → a collision test FAILs;
  - make sprint skip shallow water's factor → a test FAILs;
  - give the latched character a player-only speed bonus → `test_one_rule_set` FAILs.
- **AC3 Captures:** run with `--autopilot --capture … --capture-tick N` at 4 ticks (walking, sprinting, mid-dodge, mid-attack) on seed 1, plus one capture of the character stopped flush against a cliff or deep water. To find a spot, use `--at` from GOD-005 and search for one with a test helper. Look at each and give one line each.
- **AC4 Feel notes:** Lucid's playtest is the real gate (PLAN risks). In the report, list every feel number and where it lives, so the next round can tune them quickly.
- **AC5** `bash tools/godot/export.sh` → both builds; the Linux smoke capture under xvfb-run passes.
- **AC6** `npm run check && npm test` still pass (venv activated on its own line first).
