## Why this exists
The wanderer is **the north star of overgrown's identity**: a faceless figure in a dark poncho with orange geometric borders, a gold scarf and wind-blown dark hair (`docs/art/moodboard/`, images 01, 02, 06, 09 and 12 especially). Today it is a stack of primitives. Lucid judged the previous character attempts harshly ("look ass", "like something from 2010"). This is the chance to make a character Lucid is proud of.

## The brief: what you are free to do
You are the character artist, rigger and animator. Use any technique that serves the look:
- sculpt-like modelling in headless Blender Python (bmesh, Skin and Subdivision modifiers, curves, geometry nodes, shape keys, metaballs, remesh);
- hand-painted or procedurally painted textures baked in Blender, or vertex colours;
- toon or painterly shading in Godot that matches a painted world: rim light, stepped ramps, brush-texture shading, inverted-hull or post outlines;
- a rig with as many bones as you need;
- animations keyed by script, procedural, or a mix;
- cloth and hair motion through bones driven in Godot (spring or verlet chains on the poncho hem, scarf and hair, reacting to velocity and to wind), shader vertex wind, or both.

You may start from a D8-allowed base, for example a CC0 rigged humanoid (Quaternius and similar). If you do, rework it until it is clearly the wanderer, and add a CREDITS row. Downloads may fail behind the proxy; if they do, build from scratch.

**Exhaust your ability.** Silhouette first: the wanderer must read at about 80 px tall (the default play zoom) **and** look beautiful up close in the vista. Study the mood-board images of the wanderer (Read them) and match their proportions, shapes and colours.

## The frame (hard constraints)
1. **Blender runs headless:** `blender --background --python <script>` (Blender 4.5.14 LTS is installed).
   - **Build scripts** live in `art/blender/` (`wanderer.py` plus helpers). Read `art/blender/` in `CLAUDE.md`'s layout and OQ-037.
   - **Determinism:** the build must be deterministic, so rebuilding gives byte-identical `.glb` output. The spike solved this; read `git -C /home/user/overgrown show spike-godot:spike/PROGRESS.md` and `spike-godot:spike/blender/build_character.py` for its five non-determinism workarounds.
   - **One command** rebuilds the asset: for example `bash art/blender/build.sh` or `tools/blender/build.sh`, your choice, documented.
2. **Output and commits:** the output is `game/assets/wanderer/wanderer.glb`, plus textures if any. Commit the generated `.glb` as an authored asset (D8 allows it). CI doesn't run Blender yet, so also commit the script, and prove that a rebuild reproduces the committed file.
3. **In the game:**
   - **Add a new view, `game/src/render/wanderer_view.gd`**, that reads the same rules state as `character_view.gd`: pos, facing, action, action_start, vel, and `sim.you`. It plays the right animation: idle, walk, run (sprint), dodge, attack.
     - **Attack:** GOD-008 is changing attacks so the wanderer **keeps moving while attacking**. Make the attack an upper-body layer, or a blend that works over walk.
     - **Dodge:** a full-size roll or dash; Lucid disliked the shrinking roll.
   - **Swap it in with as small a change as possible.** Add `--wanderer old|new`, defaulting to new, in `game/scenes/main.gd`'s view setup. GOD-008 edits `main.gd` and `character_view.gd` concurrently, so keep your diff there tiny, and don't modify `character_view.gd`.
   - **Rules stay untouched** (`game/src/rules/**`, `game/src/world/**`, `contracts/**`). Views only read.
   - **Physics interpolation:** move the root in `_physics_process`, like `character_view`. Animation can advance in `_process`.
4. **Assets: D8.** Third-party files go only under `game/assets/third_party/` with a CREDITS row, and `bash tools/check-credits.sh` must pass.
5. **Keep these working:** `bash tools/godot/test.sh` → all pass, `bash tools/godot/export.sh` → both builds, and the CI smoke capture.
6. **Don't touch** the world look (ART-027 works on the post stack, sky, terrain and foliage), `camera_rig.gd` or `local_input.gd`.

## Deliverables
- **The build:** `art/blender/` scripts, the one-command build, and `game/assets/wanderer/wanderer.glb`.
- **The animations:** idle, walk, run, dodge and attack, plus secondary motion on the poncho, scarf and hair that responds to movement (and wind, if ART-027 exposes a wind direction; otherwise a constant is fine).
- **The view:** `game/src/render/wanderer_view.gd` and the `--wanderer` flag.
- **Proof of determinism:** build twice → identical `sha256`.
- **`docs/art/wanderer/`:**
  - `PROGRESS.md`, a running log of what you tried, rejected and why;
  - turnaround renders (front, three-quarter, back), Blender or Godot captures;
  - an animation contact sheet (frames from each animation in a grid);
  - in-game captures at the default zoom and close up, walking, mid-dodge and mid-attack-while-walking;
  - side-by-sides with the mood-board wanderer images.
- **Your final report:**
  - what you built;
  - the bone and triangle counts;
  - how secondary motion works;
  - what you'd do next;
  - the 4–6 best images as absolute paths.

## Working method
- **Commit and push often:** `git -C /home/user/wt/g1-wanderer-lab push -q origin g1-wanderer-lab`, retrying up to 4 times. Keep `PROGRESS.md` current so a restarted session can resume.
