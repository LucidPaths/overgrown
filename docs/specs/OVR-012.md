## Locked decision: new row **OQ-045** "Dungeon death (2026-10-20)", resolved
Dying in a dungeon or rift:
1. **Teleport.** You're teleported to the entrance, as today. There is **no XP loss and no de-leveling** any more.
2. **A sealed ability.** One of the character's abilities (or draft picks) is **sealed**: it can't be used until a **church or healer NPC unseals it**.
   - *Decided in its task:* which ability is sealed, and what unsealing costs (gold, an offering, a small quest).
   - *Open, also for that task:* whether more deaths seal more.
3. **World difficulty rises, permanently.** Every dungeon death raises a **world-wide difficulty level** for everyone in that world. It **never goes back down**.
   - *Decided in its task:* what it scales (creature levels, density, both); by how much; how it combines with distance (OQ-043).
4. **That dungeon gets harder too.** The existing rule (07-dungeons, DNG-009) stays as it is: that dungeon's difficulty tier rises for everyone. DNG-009's decay and its "drops on deep clears" stay unchanged; Lucid's "never" applies to the world difficulty only.
5. **No death limit per character** (considered and rejected).

**It supersedes** the dungeon-death XP-loss and de-level rule: OQ-007 if it states it, plus 05-combat, 04-characters, 07-dungeons, CMB-021, DNG-008, and CHR-003 (already struck).

**It removes** the de-level question from CHR-020's open points (there's no de-leveling any more). Update CHR-020's line and the 04-characters "not decided yet" list.

**Pillar 1 (one rule set):** NPCs that adventure in dungeons follow the same rule; NPC sealing and unsealing work the same way. Record this as the default per pillar 1. Whether NPC deaths raise world difficulty is decided in the world-difficulty task. Don't decide it here.
