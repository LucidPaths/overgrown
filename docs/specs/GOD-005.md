# GOD-005: Chunk streaming, 3D terrain and the tilted camera

Plan: `docs/overhaul/PLAN.md` § G0 ("**GOD-005** Chunk streaming plus 3D terrain (heightfield and biome materials), and the tilted ARPG camera with a vista zoom") and D4.
Milestone: G0   Depends on: GOD-004 (`game/src/world/*.gd`, PR #39). Branch from `origin/god-004-worldgen`; the orchestrator merges `main` in once GOD-004 lands.
Model: opus. Threading, meshing and camera feel need judgement; the boundaries are locked below.
Branch: god-005-terrain

## Desired result
Running the game shows a seeded 3D world that streams around a focus point. The world has rolling land, sunken water with a water surface, terraced mountains with cliff steps, and colours per tile and biome. It is seen through a tilted action-RPG camera that shows a sliver of sky at the top of the frame. Holding the vista key pulls the camera out to a wide, high view. Moving the focus never freezes the game for the 120 ms one chunk costs to generate.

GOD-006 replaces the debug focus mover with the character. Everything here is the world and the camera, not the player.

## Locked design

### Units and axes
1. **One tile = one metre.**
   - Tile (tx, ty) covers world x ∈ [tx, tx+1) and z ∈ [ty, ty+1).
   - Tile y-down maps to Godot +z (south); +y is up.
   - Add `const TILE_M := 1.0` to `OgWorld`. `TILE_PX` stays only because `find_spawn` returns px; convert with `/ OgWorld.TILE_PX`.

### Rules side (no Node, no rendering; `game/src/world/`)
2. **`chunk_store.gd` (`OgChunkStore`, RefCounted)** is the one world cache that rules and the renderer read.
   - **API:**
     - `_init(seed)`;
     - `tile_at(tx, ty)`, `biome_at(tx, ty)`, `height_at(x, z)`;
     - `chunk(cx, cy) -> OgChunk`;
     - `set_anchors(points: Array[Vector2])` (world metres, x and z);
     - `step()`, called once per physics tick;
     - signals `chunk_loaded(cx, cy)` and `chunk_unloaded(cx, cy)`.
   - **On-demand lookups.** `tile_at`, `biome_at`, `height_at` and `chunk` never fail. A missing chunk is generated synchronously, as TS `tileAt` does. If that chunk is already in flight on a worker thread, wait for that task (`WorkerThreadPool.wait_for_task_completion`) instead of generating it twice.
   - **Streaming, ported from `planStream`:**
     - load radius 3 and unload beyond 4 (Chebyshev, in chunks, around each anchor's chunk);
     - load order by (d², cy, cx);
     - an LRU of `RECENT_CHUNKS = 64` unloaded chunks kept for a quick return.
     - The radii grew because the tilted camera sees farther than the old top-down view. They are tunable constants.
   - **Asynchronous generation.**
     - `step()` starts at most `MAX_JOBS_IN_FLIGHT` (start at 4) `WorkerThreadPool` tasks for planned chunks.
     - It collects the finished tasks and emits `chunk_loaded` on the main thread.
     - A task calls only pure static functions and writes only into its own result object. Nothing on a worker thread touches the store, a Node or a signal.
     - Which chunks are loaded depends only on the anchors and the calls made. *When* they load may depend on thread timing; *what* they contain never does.
   - **`OgChunk`** (inner class or its own file) holds:
     - `cx`, `cy`, and `tiles` / `biomes` (from `OgChunkGen.generate_chunk`, untouched);
     - `heights: PackedFloat32Array` with 33 × 33 corner heights in metres. Corner (lx, ly), each in 0..32, is the world corner (cx·32+lx, cy·32+ly), the top-left corner of the tile with those coordinates;
     - `water: PackedFloat32Array`, 33 × 33 water-surface heights (NaN where no water touches the corner);
     - whatever per-corner colour data the mesher needs, if it is cheaper to compute once here.
3. **`terrain.gd` (`OgTerrain`): the height rules.** These are pure static functions of (fields, world tile coords), so chunk borders are seamless *by construction*: a corner shared by two chunks is computed from the same world inputs.
   - **Tile height:** a function of the tile id and its elevation sample.
     - **Land:** `(e - OCEAN_BELOW) * LAND_SCALE_M` (start at 40).
     - **Mountains:** terraced with `CLIFF_TERRACES`, so CLIFF tiles read as steps.
     - **Ocean:** the floor sits below sea level 0 by depth band (shallow, deep, very deep).
     - **River water tiles:** carved below the land around them.
   - **Corner height:** the mean of the 4 tiles that share the corner. The ones outside the chunk are sampled directly with `sample_biome`, `classify_biome` and `pick_tile`, never read from neighbour chunks. The job does this for the chunk's 34 × 34 tile ring.
   - **Water surface:** sea level 0 over Ocean water. Over river water it sits a fixed drop below the river's banks, and it must not step at chunk borders.
   - **`height_at(x, z)`:** the ground height under a world point, interpolated across the tile with the **same triangle split as the mesh**, so a character's feet sit on the drawn surface.
   - All shaping constants are tunable and named. Keep the arithmetic to `+ - * /`, `floor`, `abs`, `min` and `max` (no `sin`, `pow` or `exp`; see the CLAUDE.md trap), so a later host and client agree.

### Render side (`game/src/render/`; reads the store, never writes it)
4. **`terrain_view.gd` (Node3D).**
   - **On `chunk_loaded`:** queue a mesh build. Build the vertex, normal, colour and index arrays in a `WorkerThreadPool` task, from the `OgChunk` data. `OgChunk` arrays are never modified after load, so reading them is safe.
   - **On the main thread:** turn finished arrays into an `ArrayMesh` plus a `MeshInstance3D`. Do at most `MESH_ADDS_PER_FRAME` (start at 2) per frame.
   - **On `chunk_unloaded`:** free the chunk's nodes. Cancelling or ignoring a still-running build for an unloaded chunk must not leak or add the mesh.
   - **Meshes:** a 32 × 32 quad grid per chunk, one shared material using vertex colours, and normals computed so lighting reads the slopes. One water mesh per chunk covers the water tiles, with a shared translucent teal material.
   - **Colours:** a small palette table per tile id, tinted per biome, taking the mood board's direction (teal water, warm gold and orange land, cool stone). It is a placeholder until G1, but it shouldn't be ugly. Blending colours at corners is enough.
5. **`camera_rig.gd` (Node3D with a `Camera3D` child).**
   - **It follows a target Node3D.** Smoothing is frame-rate independent (`1 - exp(-k*delta)` is fine here: render code, not rules).
   - **Play view:**
     - yaw fixed (north up);
     - pitch about 30° below horizontal and vertical FOV about 55°, so the top of the frame shows a sliver of sky (D4);
     - distance about 14 m, with mouse-wheel zoom clamped to 8–24 m.
   - **Vista:** while `camera_vista` is held, ease to about 55 m distance at about 38° pitch; release eases back. Tune these numbers by looking at captures.
   - **Environment:**
     - `WorldEnvironment` with a procedural sky leaning teal and gold;
     - depth fog that hides the streaming edge: terrain beyond about 3 chunks fades into the sky colour, including in vista;
     - one `DirectionalLight3D` sun with shadows.
6. **Input:** `game/src/input/actions.gd` (`OgActions`) with a static `ensure()` that registers InputMap actions in code, idempotently. GOD-006 extends this same file.
   - `move_left/right/up/down` on WASD, the arrow keys and the left stick;
   - `camera_vista` on V and gamepad Y;
   - `camera_zoom_in/out` on the mouse wheel.
7. **`main.gd` / `main.tscn`:**
   - Seed from `--seed N` (user args; default 1).
   - Spawn from `OgSpawn.find_spawn` (px → m).
   - A temporary **debug focus**: a small marker Node3D moved by the move actions at 6 m/s, with no collision, `y = store.height_at(x, z)`. Comment it as GOD-006's to replace.
   - The store's anchor is the focus, and `step()` runs in `_physics_process`.
   - The HUD label keeps version and commit, and adds seed, focus tile, biome, loaded chunk count and fps.
   - Turn on `physics/common/physics_interpolation`. The focus and camera move in `_physics_process`; this sets the pattern for GOD-006.
8. **Capture mode** (extend the existing one; keep the watchdog):
   - `--capture <png>` now waits for **world ready**: every chunk within load radius 1 of the focus is meshed, then the warm-up frames run.
   - `--vista` captures in the vista pose.
   - `--at X,Z` places the focus (world metres) before streaming starts.
   - Print the time from scene start to world ready.

### Tests (`game/tests/`)
9. **`test_chunk_store.gd`:**
   - `tile_at`/`biome_at` equal `generate_chunk` for sample tiles, including negative coordinates;
   - streaming: after enough `step()` calls the loaded set equals the Chebyshev square of radius 3; moving the anchor by one chunk loads only the new row or column and unloads nothing until beyond radius 4;
   - async results equal synchronous `generate_chunk` byte for byte;
   - `tile_at` on a chunk that is in flight returns the right tile, and the chunk is not generated twice (count generations through a test seam);
   - an unloaded chunk comes back from the LRU without regenerating.
10. **`test_terrain.gd`:**
    - corner heights on the shared edge of horizontally *and* vertically adjacent chunks are equal (exact float equality);
    - the same for water heights;
    - `height_at` at a corner equals the corner height, and inside a tile it lies within the min/max of its 4 corners;
    - water-tile corners are lower than the surrounding dry-land corners for one known river and one known coast (pick real coordinates from the fixture seeds);
    - the heights are deterministic: two stores with the same seed give identical arrays.
11. No test asserts on pixels. The captures are for eyes (AC3).

## Acceptance criteria
- **AC1** `bash tools/godot/test.sh` → all PASS, exit 0.
- **AC2** Mutations, each temporary and reverted:
  - make corner height read the chunk's own clamped edge tile instead of sampling across the border → the seam test FAILs;
  - generate twice for an in-flight chunk → the in-flight test FAILs.
- **AC3 Captures.**
  - `tools/godot/capture.sh` (or the main scene with `--capture`) for seed 1 at spawn, at a coast or river, and at a mountain, each in play view and vista: 6 PNGs.
  - Look at every one and describe in one line each what is visible.
  - Required: no holes or seams between chunks; water visibly below land with a surface; sky visible at the top in play view; the streaming edge hidden by fog in vista.
  - Put the PNGs in the PR (commit them under `docs/specs/GOD-005/` at ≤ 1280×720; this is evidence, not game assets).
- **AC4 Timing (report, no threshold):**
  - median `generate_chunk` + height job per chunk;
  - median mesh build;
  - main-thread time per mesh add;
  - scene start → world ready;
  - the longest frame while the focus moves across 5 chunks in a scripted run.
  - Main-thread work per frame must not include chunk generation, except for on-demand lookups the rules make.
- **AC5** `bash tools/godot/export.sh` → both builds export, and the Linux smoke capture works under xvfb-run as in CI.
- **AC6** `npm run check && npm test` still pass, with the venv activated on its own line first.
