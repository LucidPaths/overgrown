## Locked design

### 1. Camera orbit (view and input only; rules untouched)
- **Mouse orbit:** hold the right mouse button and drag. Horizontal drag rotates yaw freely through 360°. Vertical drag changes pitch, clamped to about 18°–60° below the horizon. The low end must still show a strip of sky; the high end is close to top-down.
- **Keys and gamepad:** Q and E rotate yaw at a steady rate. The gamepad D-pad left/right does the same. The right stick stays aim.
- **Cursor:** stays visible while orbiting. Releasing the button returns to normal aiming.
- **Mouse aim while orbiting:** pick whatever reads right. Keep the last aim, or keep aiming at the cursor.
- **Persistence:** yaw and pitch persist for the session; there's no reset needed. Vista still eases out from the current yaw.
- **Bindings:** go in `OgActions` (`camera_orbit` on the right mouse button, `camera_left`/`camera_right` on Q/E and the D-pad), documented in its comment block.
- **Camera-relative movement:** `local_input` already maps screen directions through the camera basis. Verify it with yaw ≠ 0 at 90° and 180°, and make sure the gamepad stick and the right-stick aim map through the yaw the same way.
- **Fog and streaming:** the streaming radius is a Chebyshev square. Check the fog still hides the edge when you look diagonally at 45° yaw, in play and in vista. Adjust the fog distances if not.

### 2. Attack while moving (rules change; one rule set, so NPCs get it too)
- **During ATTACK, the character moves.** Movement intent is applied exactly as in WALK, but at `ATTACK_MOVE_FACTOR` × walk speed (start at 0.85, tunable, in `OgRules`). Sprint is ignored during an attack, so it's never faster than a walk. The tile speed factor and walls apply as usual.
- **Facing** stays snapped to the attack aim for the whole attack (OQ-025). Strafing while swinging is the point.
- **The action stays ATTACK until it ends.** Attack and dodge start rules, the buffer and the durations are unchanged.
- **Tests:** update `test_character_rules.gd`. The old "attack roots" assertion becomes "attack moves at ATTACK_MOVE_FACTOR, facing held to the aim, sprint ignored, walls and shallow water still apply". `test_one_rule_set` must still pass unchanged.
- **Mutation:** re-rooting the attack must FAIL the new test.
- **Rules arithmetic** stays as it is: `+ − × ÷`, sqrt, floor, abs, min, max.

### 3. Dodge visual (view only)
- **No body scaling during the dodge.** Drop the `TUCK_SCALE` shrink. The wanderer stays full size.
- **Reads clearly at the default 14 m zoom** (about 80 px tall): for example, a full-size forward roll or dive along the velocity, a low dash lean with a short motion trail or afterimage, or a dust puff at the start. The choice is yours; judge it on captures at the default zoom.
- **Lands back upright** at the end of `DODGE_TICKS`, with no pop.
- Physics interpolation stays correct (reset only on teleports).
