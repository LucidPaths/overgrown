# 09 — Housing, Camps & Taming

## Claim and furnish

- Houses in settlements can be **bought or rented** (vacant ones, including ones freed when an NPC dies or migrates).
- Furnish with crafted or bought furniture: bed (for sleeping, WLD-010; not a respawn point, OQ-042), storage, crafting stations, decorations.
- A home belongs to the character in that world. If the settlement dies, so does your neighborhood.
- No free-form base building for now. It may come after MVP (OQ-010).

## Camps

- Set up a camp anywhere: bedroll, campfire (cooking and light), small storage, a basic workbench.
- Camps are temporary. Monsters can wreck them, and they decay when left.
- A bedroll at night lets you sleep the night away (→ WLD-010).

## Taming

- **Taming trait**, grown by attempting and succeeding at taming.
- Each species has a taming method: feed it, calm it, or tame it young. Bigger, older or meaner creatures need a higher trait rank.
- **Tamed creatures** follow, fight, guard camps or homes, and some can be **ridden** (horse-likes, big birds on the ground, large cats).
- Tamed creatures still age and can die. Breeding tamed creatures produces offspring that are easier to tame.
