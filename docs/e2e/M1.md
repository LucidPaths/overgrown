How to read it:
- **Ground costs half the fps in this environment** (60 → 30), and it does so standing still (30.1 fps) as much as walking (27.5–31.8). That is the SwiftShader fill cost of the ground sprites, not streaming. This is the "headless halves fps" effect; it says nothing about a real GPU.
- **No frame over 100 ms in any run** (the 100 ms maxima equal 6 vsyncs; the standing ground run and the W *baseline* run have the same 100 ms frame, so it is run-to-run noise). The worst walking frame (66.8–100 ms) is within the baseline's own worst (66.6–100 ms) for the same noise level.
- **Chunk arrivals cost a little.** Of the frames > 50 ms, 6–17 % fall in the 150 ms after a chunk batch, windows that cover 2.5–3.7 % of the time (E 5/30, N 4/37, S 7/55, W 9/146). So a bake adds about one missed vsync at 30 fps, never more. Frames > 50 ms per second: E 0.74, N 0.58, S 1.4, W 2.2, standing 0.8 (the S/W spread is mostly noise: S ran at the quietest load, W at the busiest).
- **Streaming invariants,** replayed from the recorded `chunk`/`unchunk` message log against the player's chunk at every snapshot (`stream_check`): the 5×5 chunks around the player were always loaded within 118–128 ms of the player entering the new chunk (max 3 chunks missing for ≤ 3 ticks while a 5-chunk column arrived at 2 per tick); the 3×3 around the player was never missing; **no chunk beyond Chebyshev distance 3 was ever kept** (max 0); end state 30 held chunks. 50 loads + 50 unloads per 10-chunk straight line (55 + 55 for the 11 crossings of the walks).
- JS heap: 10.0 → 10.0 MB (baseline) and 11.9 → 11.9 / 11.2 → 11.2 MB (ground; `performance.memory` is coarse) across 5120 px: no growth seen. No console errors in any run.
- **Holes:** a separate ground run (`S`, sprint) took 20 full screenshots while moving (about every 1.8 s) and counted pixels equal to the background colour `#1d2b22` outside the debug-text box: 0 in all 20. `10-walk10-sprint-south-mid.png`, `11-walk10-sprint-south-end.png`.

## Observations and risks (not failures)

1. **First-frame margin is 246 ms.** Passes OQ-028 but a content addition could push the median past 3 s; TEC-012 (≤ 2 s) is the planned fix. Worth re-measuring after each M2 asset batch.
2. **Ground draw halves software-GL fps and every chunk bake costs about one missed vsync.** Standing still gives 30 fps vs 60 with no ground, and frames > 50 ms cluster 3–4× above chance right after chunk batches (1 bake per frame, `BAKES_PER_FRAME`). Never worse than 100 ms here. Not verified on a real GPU; if it matters, a real-device check belongs with the next perf pass.
3. **Mountain is a flat grey plain.** In seed 6 the Mountain biome is a ~120×120-tile field of uniform Stone with a few cliff strips and, at its edge, a ~28-tile perfectly straight vertical border (elevation changes 0.0015 per tile there, so the threshold contour is a straight line). Walkable and distinct, but monotonous; a worldgen/art note, not a bug.
4. **Wading has no visual feedback.** The character is drawn identically on land and in shallow water (and in the river); only the speed changes. Art/UX (ART-024 or later).
5. **Art, briefly:** deep water and the Mountain stone are both blue-grey from a distance; the ocean shelf next to spawn in seed 42 is an 80-tile wadeable shallow (deep water exists, e.g. seed 1 at 154 tiles away).
6. **By design, noted so nobody files them:** sprint reuses the walk animation (no sprint-speed anim); a dodge faces the mouse aim; seeds 0, 2, 4294967295 (and many more) spawn at tile (0, 0).
7. **E2E tooling:** there is no debug/test hook on the page; this run wrapped `Worker` from an init script to read snapshots and chunk messages. A tiny opt-in `?debug` state export would make later milestone E2E (M2 combat) simpler and less fragile, but is not needed for M1.
